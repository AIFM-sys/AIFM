Ek chhote se gaon mein, ek purana sa ghar tha. Us ghar ki deewarein thodi thodi toot chuki theen, chhat se kabhi kabhi mitti girti, aur raat ko hawa jab guzarti to aisa lagta jaise ghar khud saans le raha ho. Us ghar mein ek bohat chalak billi rehti thi. Us ka naam tha Munni. Munni ki aankhein tezz, kaan hamesha hoshiyar, aur chal itni khamosh ke jaise saya chal raha ho. Munni ko apni chalaki par bohat ghuroor tha. Woh sochti thi ke is ghar mein jo bhi chuha hoga, woh us ke samne tik nahi sakta.

Isi ghar ke andar, deewar ke peeche ek chhota sa suraakh tha. Us suraakh mein rehta tha ek chuha, jis ka naam tha Guddu. Guddu chhota zaroor tha, magar dimagh us ka bohat tez tha. Woh har cheez ko dekh kar samajhta, har awaz ko sun kar andaza lagata, aur har raat ko khana dhoondhne nikalta. Guddu ko pata tha ke ghar mein billi rehti hai, aur woh chahti hai ke woh ek din us ko pakar le. Magar Guddu ne apne dil mein ek baat pakki kar li thi ke main sirf apni chalaki se nahi, apni samajh se bachunga.

Ek raat, jab sab log so gaye, kitchen mein thodi roti ka tukra aur thoda sa chawal pada tha. Guddu ko bohat bhook lagi thi. Woh apne suraakh se bahar nikal aaya. Pehle us ne apni naak hawa mein utha kar soongha. Hawa mein roti ki khushboo thi. Woh khushi se aage barha. Lekin us ne deewar ki taraf nazar bhi rakhi, kyun ke Munni ka haqeeqi kamal yahi tha ke woh achanak kahin se bhi nikal aati.

Guddu jese hi chawal ke qareeb pohncha, us ne ek halki si khar khar ki awaz suni, jaise kisi ne apna panja zameen par ghaseeta ho. Guddu ka dil ek pal ko dhak se reh gaya. Woh bilkul ruk gaya. Us ne apni aankhein idhar udhar ghumain. Chulhe ke neeche andhera tha. Bartan ke peechay saaya sa hilta mehsoos hua. Aur phir… Munni bilkul khamoshi se bahar nikal aayi.

Munni ne muskurate hue kaha, “Ahaaa  Guddu  Aaj tum mere haath aa gaye.”

Guddu ne bhi apni hoshiyari se kaam liya. Woh ghabraya nahi. Us ne aisa dikhaya jaise woh bohat darr gaya ho. Phir woh dheere se bola, “Billi baji  mujhe pakar hi lo, lekin ek baat bata do  tum itni tez kaise ho  tumharay paas koi raaz hai na”

Munni ko apni tareef sun kar maza aaya. Billi ko jab apni shaan ki baat mile, toh woh zyada hoshiyar nahi rehti. Munni ne gardan upar ki aur kaha, “Raaz  mera raaz mera tajurbah hai. Main saalon se shikar kar rahi hoon. Main har chuhe ki chal pehchan leti hoon.”

Guddu ne foran kaha, “Wah  phir to tum is ghar ki malik ho  tum se behtar koi nahi. Bas ek chhoti si guzarish hai. Pakarne se pehle mujhe do dafa poori kitchen dekh lene do. Main zindagi mein shayad dobara itni badi kitchen na dekh sakoon.”

Munni hansi. “Dekho dekho  tum chuhe bhi kitne ajeeb ho. Chalo theek hai. Lekin zyada hoshiyari ki to seedha mere munh mein.”

Guddu dheere dheere chalne laga. Woh kitchen ke ek kone ki taraf gaya, phir doosre kone. Us ka dimagh tezi se soch raha tha. Woh koi raasta dhoondh raha tha. Us ne dekha ke kitchen ke darwazay ke paas ek purani jhaaru rakhi hai, aur us ke sath ek dabba bhi pada hai. Us dabbe ke andar shayad dal ya aata tha. Guddu ko ek plan soojha.

Woh jhaaru ke paas gaya aur jaan boojh kar us se takra gaya. Jhaaru hilli, aur dabba bhi thoda sa sarak gaya. Dabbe ka dhakkan theek se band nahi tha. Dabbe ke andar se thoda sa aata nikal kar zameen par gir gaya. Munni ne yeh dekha aur aankhein tang kar ke boli, “Kya kar rahe ho  abhi main tumhe pakar lungi.”

Guddu ne masoomiyat se kaha, “Maaf kar do  meri taangein kaanp rahi hain. Main bohat darr gaya hoon.”

Munni aage barhi. Ab woh bilkul qareeb thi. Guddu ne ek dum se aata par apne paon maar kar aata hawa mein uchaal diya. Aata Munni ki aankhon aur naak par ja laga. Munni chhinkne lagi, aankhein band kar ke panjay se apna chehra saaf karne lagi. Bas Guddu ko yahi pal chahiye tha.

Guddu tezi se bhaaga. Munni ne ghusse mein miaaaoo ki, “Ruko  tum bach nahi sakte.” Munni ne peechay daud lagayi. Guddu seedha kitchen ke darwazay ki taraf bhaaga, magar wahan se bahar jaana asaan nahi tha kyun ke darwaza band tha. Guddu ne apne dimagh mein jaldi se naya raasta dhoondha. Us ne dekha ke deewar ke neeche ek chhota sa suraakh hai jo veranda ki taraf jata hai, lekin woh suraakh thoda tang hai. Guddu us mein ghus sakta tha, Munni nahi.

Guddu us suraakh ki taraf lapka. Munni qareeb aayi, lekin us ka panja suraakh ke andar atak gaya. Guddu bach gaya. Munni ne panja zor se nikala aur ghusse se boli, “Chalak chuha  lekin main tumhe chhorungi nahi.”

Guddu veranda mein aa gaya. Veranda mein thandi hawa chal rahi thi. Chaand ki roshni zameen par bikhri hui thi. Guddu ko lag raha tha jaise aaj woh zinda bach kar koi jang jeet aaya ho. Magar us ne apne dil mein kaha, “Sirf bach jana jeet nahi hoti. Aaj Munni ko meri chalaki ka pata chal gaya. Kal woh double hoshiyar hogi.”

Agli subah, ghar ke log uthay. Aurat ne kitchen saaf karte hue dekha ke aata zameen par bikhra hua hai. Woh boli, “Hayee  raat ko chuhe phir aa gaye.” Us ne jhaaru uthayi, aata sameta, aur dabba theek se band kar diya.

Munni aik kone mein bethi, apna gussa andar daba rahi thi. Us ki aankhon mein aag thi. Woh soch rahi thi, “Guddu ne mujhe bewaqoof banaya. Ab main us ke liye aisi chaal chalungi ke woh soch bhi nahi sakta.”

Raat hui. Guddu apne suraakh mein bethi bethi soch raha tha ke ab kya karna hai. Bhook phir se lagi. Magar is dafa woh bina sochay bahar nahi nikla. Us ne pehle sun’na shuru kiya. Kitchen se koi awaz nahi aa rahi thi. Magar kabhi kabhi khamoshi bhi ek jaal hoti hai. Guddu ne apne dost, ek chhoti si chipkali ko awaaz di, “Suno  Zara kitchen ka chakkar laga kar aao, dekho koi billi to nahi.”

Chipkali diwaar par chalti hui kitchen mein gayi, dekh kar wapas aayi, aur boli, “Kitchen mein Munni nazar nahi aa rahi. Magar darwazay ke paas ek nayi si cheez rakhi hai, jaise koi kapra ya thaila.”

Guddu samajh gaya. “Yeh jaal ho sakta hai.” Woh bahar nikla. Dheere dheere, bohat hoshiyari se, kitchen mein enter hua. Darwazay ke paas sach mein ek kapray ka thaila pada tha, jo thoda sa hil bhi raha tha. Guddu ne socha, “Yahan Munni chhupi hui hai.” Us ne apne paas se ek chhota sa tukra, jo us ne din mein chura kar rakha tha, zameen par phainka. Us tukray ki awaz hui.

Achanak thailay se Munni bahar nikal aayi. “Ahaaa  pakar liya tumhe.”

Lekin Guddu wahan tha hi nahi. Guddu to deewar ke sath sath chal raha tha, aur thaila sirf distraction tha. Munni ne gusse se idhar udhar dekha. Guddu ne usi waqt bartanon ke peeche se ek chhota sa biscuit ka tukra uthaya aur phir seedha apne suraakh ki taraf bhaaga.

Munni ne dekh liya, aur phir daud lagayi. Is dafa Munni ne socha tha ke main us suraakh ko pehle se block kar dungi. Munni ne wahan ek chhota sa pathar rakh diya tha. Guddu jab suraakh ke qareeb pohncha to pathar dekh kar ruk gaya. Us ka dil dobara dhak se reh gaya. Munni qareeb thi. Ab waqt bohat kam tha.

Guddu ne phir ek nayi soch sochi. Us ne biscuit ka tukra Munni ki taraf phainka. Munni ne reflex mein us tukray ko pakarne ki koshish ki, aur us ka sar thoda sa side par hua. Guddu ne isi pal deewar par latki hui purani rassi ko dekha, jo shayad kisi zamane mein saman latkane ke liye use hoti thi. Guddu ne rassi ko pakra aur apne chhote se jism ko kheench kar upar chadhaya. Munni ne panja mara, magar Guddu rassi par upar ja chuka tha.

Guddu rassi par chadh kar shelf tak pohnch gaya. Shelf par ek chhota sa suraakh tha jo seedha doosre kamray mein khulta tha. Guddu us mein ghus gaya aur bach gaya.

Munni neeche khari, pehle hairan, phir ghusse mein. “Tum bohat tez ho  lekin main bhi haar nahi manungi.”

Guddu ne andar se aahista se kaha, “Munni baji  main tumhari dushmani nahi chahta. Main bas jeena chahta hoon. Tum bhi pet ke liye shikar karti ho, main bhi pet ke liye khana dhoondta hoon. Magar kya hum har raat jang karte rahenge”

Munni chup ho gayi. Us ne pehli dafa socha, “Yeh chuha sirf bhaagta nahi, yeh baat bhi samajhta hai.” Munni ko yaad aaya ke jab woh chhoti thi, us ke paas bhi koi nahi tha. Us ne bhi apni zindagi apni samajh se banayi.

Agle din, ghar ke malik ne ek faisla kiya. Us ne bola, “Chuho ne bohat tang kiya. Main kal se billi ko bahar chhor kar aunga aur ek pinjra bhi lagaunga.”

Munni ne yeh suna to us ka dil bhi halka sa bhari ho gaya. “Agar main bahar chali gayi, to main bhi is ghar ki roti se door. Aur Guddu agar pinjray mein phans gaya to zinda nahi bachega.”

Raat ko Munni chup chap Guddu ke suraakh ke paas aayi. Guddu dara nahi, kyun ke Munni ne aaj daud nahi lagayi. Munni ne aahista se kaha, “Guddu  kal ghar wale pinjra laga rahe hain. Tumhe sambhal kar rehna.”

Guddu hairan reh gaya. “Tum mujhe warn kyun kar rahi ho”

Munni ne dheere se kaha, “Main shikari hoon, magar main zulm nahi chahti. Tum bhi is ghar ka hissa ho. Aur sach bataun  tumhari chalaki ne mujhe kuch sikhaya bhi hai. Ke har jang mein jeetna zaroori nahi hota.”

Guddu ki aankhon mein chamak aa gayi. “Main tumhara ehsaan nahi bhoolunga. Main kal se apna raasta badal dunga.”

Agli subah, jab pinjra laga, Guddu us raat kitchen mein gaya hi nahi. Woh ghar ke doosre hisse mein, jahan kam log aate the, wahan se thoda sa khana dhoondh kar laaya. Munni ne bhi ghar walon ko yeh mehsoos karwaya ke chuhe kam ho gaye hain. Kuch din guzray. Ghar wale khush ho gaye. Unhon ne pinjra hata diya. Munni ko bahar chhorne ka irada bhi thanda pad gaya.

Waqt guzarta gaya. Munni aur Guddu ki jang khatam nahi hui, magar ab woh jang itni sakht nahi thi. Kabhi kabhi Munni bas Guddu ko darra deti, aur Guddu apni chalaki se nikal jata. Dono ko pata tha ke zindagi mein har rishte ka naam dosti nahi hota, lekin har rishta dushmani bhi nahi hota.

Ek raat, Guddu apne suraakh mein betha tha, us ke paas thoda sa biscuit tha, thoda sa dana. Woh muskuraya aur socha, “Main zinda hoon. Aur zinda rehne ka matlab hai samajh, sabr, aur waqt par sahi faisla.”

Aur Munni apni jagah par bethi thi. Us ne bhi socha, “Main bhi zinda hoon. Aur zinda rehna sirf shikar nahi, kabhi kabhi kisi ko jeene dena bhi hota hai.”

Is tarah, us purane ghar mein, billi aur chuha dono ne ek naya sabak seekha  ke chalaki se zyada powerful cheez hoti hai aqal aur dil.

AIFM stands for Application-Integrated Far Memory. It provides a simple, general, and high-performance mechanism for users to adapt ordinary memory-intensive applications to far memory. Different from existing paging-based systems, AIFM exposes far memory as far-memory pointers and containers in the language level. AIFM's API allows its runtime to accurately capture application semantics, therefore making intelligent decisions on data placement and movement.

Currently, AIFM supports C++ and TCP-enabled remote server memory.

## Supported Platform

We strongly recommend you to run AIFM using the xl170 instance of [Cloudlab](https://www.cloudlab.us/) as the code has been throughly tested there. We haven't done any test in other hardware environment. If you have trouble applying an cloudlab account, please contact us for assistance.

## Build Instructions
### Configure Cloudlab Instances

1) Apply a Cloudlab account if you do not have one.

2) Now you have logged into Cloublab console. Click `Experiments`|-->`Create Experiment Profile`. Upload `cloudlab.profile` provided in this repo root.

3) Create a two-node instance using the profile. 

### Install Dependencies (on all nodes)
Now you have logged into your Cloudlab instances. You have to install the necessary dependencies in order to build AIFM. Note you have to do run those steps on all Cloudlab nodes you have created.

1) Update package database and Linux kernel version.
```
sudo apt-get update
echo Y | sudo apt-get install linux-headers-5.0.0-20 linux-headers-5.0.0-20-generic linux-hwe-edge-tools-5.0.0-20 linux-image-5.0.0-20-generic linux-modules-5.0.0-20-generic linux-tools-5.0.0-20-generic
sudo reboot
```

2) Set bash as the default shell.
```
chsh -s /bin/bash
```

3) Install Mellanox OFED.
```
wget "http://content.mellanox.com/ofed/MLNX_OFED-4.6-1.0.1.1/MLNX_OFED_LINUX-4.6-1.0.1.1-ubuntu18.04-x86_64.tgz"
tar xvf MLNX_OFED_LINUX-4.6-1.0.1.1-ubuntu18.04-x86_64.tgz
cd MLNX_OFED_LINUX-4.6-1.0.1.1-ubuntu18.04-x86_64
sudo ./mlnxofedinstall --add-kernel-support --dpdk --upstream-libs # it's fine to see 'Failed to install libibverbs-dev DEB'
sudo /etc/init.d/openibd restart
```

4) Install libraries and tools.
```
sudo apt-get update
echo Y | sudo apt-get --fix-broken install
echo Y | sudo apt-get install libnuma-dev libmnl-dev libnl-3-dev libnl-route-3-dev
echo Y | sudo apt-get install libcrypto++-dev libcrypto++-doc libcrypto++-utils
echo Y | sudo apt-get install software-properties-common
echo Y | sudo apt-get install gcc-9 g++-9 python-pip
echo Y | sudo add-apt-repository ppa:ubuntu-toolchain-r/test
echo Y | sudo apt-get purge cmake
sudo pip install --upgrade pip
sudo pip install cmake
```


### Build Shenango and AIFM (on all nodes)
For all nodes, clone our github repo in a same path, say, your home directory. 

AIFM relies on Shenango's threading and TCP runtime. The `build_all.sh` script in repo root compiles both Shenango and AIFM automatically.
```
./build_all.sh
```

### Setup Shenango (on all nodes)
After rebooting machines, you have to rerun the script to setup Shenango.
```
sudo ./scripts/setup_machine.sh
```

### Configure AIFM (only on the compute node)
So far you have built AIFM on both nodes. One node is used as the compute node to run applications, while the other node is used as the remote memory node. Now edit `aifm/configs/ssh` in the compute node; change `MEM_SERVER_SSH_IP` to the IP of the remote memory node (eno49 inet in `ifconfig`), and `MEM_SERVER_SSH_USER` to your ssh username. Please make sure the compute node can ssh the remote memory node successfully without password.

## Run AIFM Tests
Now you are able to run AIFM programs. `aifm/test` contains a bunch of test files of using local/far pointers and containers (and few other system components). You can also treat those tests as examples of using AIFM. `aifm/test.sh` is a script that runs all tests automatically. It includes the commands of running AIFM end-to-end.
```
./test.sh
```

## Reproduce Experiment Results
We provide code and scripts in `aifm/exp` folder for reproducing our experiments. For more details, see `aifm/exp/README.md`.

## Known Limitations
AIFM is a research prototype rather than a production-ready system. Its current implementation has two main limitations.
1. A thread cannot have more than one live `DerefScope` at any time (but you can have multiple live `DerefScope`s across different threads). For example, when executing `foo()->bar()` in a thread, if you've already instantiated a `DerefScope` in `foo()`, you must not instantiate another one in `bar()`. The right way is to pass the one in `foo()` as a reference to `bar()`.
2. AIFM assumes that the __remote__ memory is sufficiently large so that it never garbage collects the dead (i.e., freed) objects in the __remote__ memory, see `FarMemManager::mutator_wait_for_gc_far_mem()` in `aifm/src/manager.cpp`. However, AIFM does garbage collects the dead objects in the __local__ memory since it's a precious resource.
